Findings:
- **ReadsPerGene.out.tab** (manual section 8, pages 18 to 19):
  - Column 1 is the gene ID.
  - Column 2 counts unstranded reads.
  - Column 3 counts reads whose 1st read strand aligns with the RNA, "htseq-count option -s yes".
  - Column 4 counts reads whose 2nd read strand aligns with the RNA, "-s reverse".
  - A read is counted only if it overlaps one and only one gene by at least 1 nt. When you use column 3 or 4, the other column holds the antisense reads.
  - The manual does not describe the four N_ rows. The source does (`Transcriptome.cpp` `quantsOutput()` and `Transcriptome_geneCountsAddAlign.cpp` at tag 2.7.11b):
    - N_unmapped = unmappedMismatch + unmappedShort + unmappedOther + unmappedMulti. This includes "mapped to too many loci".
    - N_multimapping = cMulti, meaning reads with more than 1 alignment. It is the same value in all three columns.
    - N_noFeature and N_ambiguous are counted per column.

  - The 101 kb fixture gives 7.31, so 7.
