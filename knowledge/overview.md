# Cancer Transcriptomics Teaching Suite — overview

Ten lines, for someone who has never seen this project. If it takes more than
that, the project is doing two things and should be two repositories.

**Question.** What this project is trying to find out.

**Input.** What data comes in, from where, and who produced it.

**Method.** The two or three steps that matter, in order. Everything else is in
`codebase_map.md`, which is generated and always current.

**Output.** What leaves the project, and who consumes it — a manuscript figure,
a downstream repository, a collaborator.

**Status.** Exploratory / in analysis / frozen for submission. Say which.

---

Generated files worth opening first: `explain_codebase.html` (run order and
dataflow), `api_index.md` (every function, this project's and `labcore`'s).
Neither is hand-written, so neither can be out of date.
